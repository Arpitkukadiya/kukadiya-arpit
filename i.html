How we work

From first call to launch day.

A clear seven-step process that keeps every project on track — no guesswork, no surprises.

01

Client Visit — Requirements

We meet (in person or on a call) to understand your business, your audience, and what success means for this site.

02

Design

I create wireframes and visual mockups so you can see and approve the look before a single line of code is written.

03

Functionality

The design comes to life — responsive layouts, booking flows, forms, dashboards, and everything you need to run the site.

04

Testing

Cross-browser checks, mobile testing, speed optimization, and edge-case QA — before you ever see a bug.

05

Client Visit — Reviews

We walk through the finished build together. You give feedback; I take notes and align on the final changes.

06

Changes & Revisions

Your feedback gets implemented — quickly and without extra back-and-forth. We iterate until it's right.

07

Production Ready

Site goes live, domains point to the right place, and you get a handoff document with everything you need to run it.
